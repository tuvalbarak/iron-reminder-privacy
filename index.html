Iron Reminder

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 23, 2026
Bundle ID: com.tuvalbarak.ironreminder
Published by Tuval Barak

Iron Reminder is a simple iPhone app that helps you remember to take a daily iron pill. This policy explains what the app does — and does not — do with information on your device.

Information the app stores

Iron Reminder stores only the dates a dose was marked taken. That information stays on your device, in the app’s local storage (UserDefaults). It is used solely to stop reminders once you have taken the day’s pill and to show whether today’s dose is done.

Notifications

The app schedules local notifications on your device only. It does not send push notifications from a server.

What we do not collect

Iron Reminder has no account and does not collect, transmit, or share personal data. In particular, the app has:

  • No network calls
  • No analytics
  • No advertising
  • No third-party SDKs
  • No tracking

Data sharing

We do not sell, share, or transfer any personal data, because the app does not collect any.

Children

Iron Reminder is intended for a general audience. It is not directed at children under 13, and it does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Deleting your data

Because all stored information stays on your device, deleting the app removes all of it.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.

Contact

For questions about this policy or the app, please get in touch via the support link on the App Store listing.
